# The Hesper Point Light

## 1. Bertil Aum keeps the Hesper Point light for an ocean that never arrived

_Year 212 of the Keth colony, late autumn, dusk · Hesper Point lighthouse, Keth salt basin · author A_

The Hesper Point lighthouse stands on a white salt basin on Keth, built 212 years ago on the terraforming charter's promise that the comet-water would fill the basin "within one generation." The ocean never arrived, but the keeper's contract was never voided, so Bertil Aum, sixty-one and the fourth keeper, still lights the lamp every dusk and logs "sea state: nil" in a ledger whose earliest pages are in the founder's hand. He polishes the brass of a maritime radio no one has ever called, out of a habit he calls superstition and his late wife called faith.

## 2. A mayday on channel sixteen, in an accent no one on Keth speaks

_Year 212, late autumn, 02:40 the same night · Lamp room, Hesper Point lighthouse · author A_

At 02:40 the never-used radio crackles on channel sixteen: "Mayday, mayday, this is the brigantine Corrigan's Patience, hull breached, taking on water, request bearing to harbor." The voice gives a latitude that is exactly Hesper Point's, and pronounces it with rolled vowels from a language Bertil only knows from the founder's ledger. He answers by the book, his hands shaking, and the voice thanks him by name—"Keeper Aum"—though he never gave it.

## 3. At dawn, a wooden ship wallows in the salt flat as if it were water

_Year 212, late autumn, dawn after the mayday · Keth salt basin, seaward of Hesper Point · author A_

When the sun clears the basin rim, Bertil sees a two-masted ship listing a kilometre out, its hull sunk to the waterline in salt that ripples around it like a swell. Behind it a dark stripe of real brine is spreading across the flat, steaming in the cold, and it smells of iodine and rot. Figures on the deck are bailing with buckets, flinging white crystals over the rail, and one of them raises an arm toward the lighthouse as though they have been following his lamp all night.

## 15. Halfway out: kelp on a dry planet, and the name on the transom
